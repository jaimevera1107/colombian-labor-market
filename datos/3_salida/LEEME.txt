Every "icml rebuild" or "icml update" creates here a folder YYYY-MM-DD_HHMMSS_cutoff-YYYY-MM_<mode>
(mode: anchored or total) with universe_census/ and universe_geih/, each with labor/, informality/ and sectoral/,
plus MANIFEST.json, VERIFICATION.json and INTEGRAL_RECOMPUTE_REPORT.json.
The simple files (basic columns, CSV and XLSX) are written next to it as <folder>_simple; "icml delivery simple --folder <folder>" rebuilds them.
"icml plots --folder <folder>" creates <folder>_plots with dashboards and comparatives (optional).
The latest delivery is the reference of the next recalculation. Deliveries are generated locally and ignored by Git.
