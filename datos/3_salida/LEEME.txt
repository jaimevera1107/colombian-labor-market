Every "icml rebuild" or "icml update" creates here a folder YYYY-MM-DD_HHMMSS_corte-YYYY-MM_<mode>
(mode: anclado or total) with universo_censo/ and universo_geih/, each with laboral/, informalidad/ and sectorial/,
plus MANIFEST.json, VERIFICACION.json and INFORME_RECALCULO_INTEGRAL.json.
The simple files (basic columns, CSV and XLSX) are written next to it as <folder>_sencillo; "icml delivery simple --folder <folder>" rebuilds them.
"icml plots --folder <folder>" creates <folder>_graficos with dashboards and comparatives (optional).
The latest delivery is the reference of the next recalculation. Deliveries are generated locally and ignored by Git.
